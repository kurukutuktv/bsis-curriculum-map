About this page

  • All text and codes in the map are transcribed from CMO No. 25, s. 2015 (Curriculum Map for BSIS, pp. 25–28; Graduate Outcomes, pp. 7–8). Wording is kept as printed, including the CMO's own typos; only spacing and punctuation spacing were tidied, and year-standing labels were written in one style (e.g. “4th Year Standing”).
  • The map lists 21 courses. The recommended ADV electives are not part of the CMO's BSIS map; an elective can still have a roadmap (it is labelled “elective”).
  • Where the CMO prints one set of codes for a whole course, the course is shown with a single set. Where it prints codes per learning outcome, the course row shows every code its outcomes carry, and the course panel shows them per outcome.
  • Typos in the CMO that are kept as printed: PILO IS08 (“techincal”, “neogitation”); IS103 LO2 (“Examine, test and evaluation web solutions”); CC104 LO1 (“a given specifications”); IS104 LO1 (“opportunites”).
  • The last outcome column of the BSIS map is headed “IT10” in the CMO; it is the BSIS outcome IS10 (Life-Long Learning) and is labelled IS10 here.
  • The Practicum is coded “PRAC01” in the CMO map. Elsewhere in the same CMO it appears as “PRAC101” (curriculum chart, p. 20) and “PRAC104” (course list, p. 17).
  • Roadmaps come from each course's OBTL and are separate from the CMO map data. Search also looks at roadmap topics, and says so when that is why a course matched.
